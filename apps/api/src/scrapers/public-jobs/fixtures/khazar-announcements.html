Jobs and Vacancies

Coordinator – Department of Economics and Management

Khazar University is seeking an experienced and academically engaged professional to join the School of Economics and Management as Department Coordinator

Key Responsibilities

  • Coordinate day-to-day academic and administrative activities of the department;
  • Support faculty members with academic planning, course coordination, and departmental processes;
  • Assist with curriculum development, course scheduling, and academic documentation;
  • Coordinate communication between faculty members, students, and relevant University units;
  • Support the organization of departmental meetings, events, seminars, and academic activities;
  • Assist with research, academic partnership, and departmental development initiatives;
  • Monitor assigned tasks and follow up on deadlines and deliverables;
  • Prepare relevant reports, documents, and correspondence;
  • Contribute to a collaborative, professional, and positive departmental environment;
  • Undertake other duties related to departmental and University development as assigned.

Qualifications & Experience

  • Ph.D. degree or currently in the dissertation defense stage in Economics, Finance, Business Administration, Management, or another relevant field;
  • 2+ years of relevant academic or professional experience;
  • Strong command of English, both written and spoken;
  • Strong organizational, communication, and interpersonal skills;
  • Ability to coordinate multiple tasks and meet deadlines;
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to work collaboratively with faculty and administrative teams;
  • Commitment to academic quality, teamwork, and continuous improvement.

Applicants who meet the above requirements are invited to submit their CV to economics@khazar.org indication “Coordinator, Department of Economics and management”

Application deadline: October 9, 2026

Only shortlisted candidates whose qualifications meet the requirements of the position will be contacted for further recruitment process.

 

Director – Department of Economics and Management

Khazar University is seeking an experienced and academically engaged professional to join the School of Economics and Management as Department Director.

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide academic and administrative leadership for the Department of Economics and Management;
  • Lead the development and continuous improvement of academic programs and curricula in line with international standards and emerging trends;
  • Promote research activity and support publications in reputable international academic journals;
  • Develop academic and professional relationships with universities, industry, government institutions, and other relevant stakeholders;
  • Contribute to international academic partnerships and collaborative initiatives;
  • Support the strategic development and implementation of departmental goals in alignment with the University's overall strategy;
  • Represent the department in relevant academic and professional activities;
  • Prepare and provide relevant reports, proposals, and undertake other responsibilities related to the University's development as assigned by University leadership.
  • Support faculty development, collaboration, and a positive collegial working environment;

Qualifications & Experience

  • Ph.D. or equivalent doctoral degree in Economics, Finance, Business Administration, Management, or a related field;
  • 5+ years of relevant academic or professional experience;
  • Excellent command of English;
  • Demonstrated research and publication experience in reputable academic journals;
  • Experience in curriculum development aligned with international academic standards;
  • Strong leadership, organizational, communication, and interpersonal skills;
  • Ability to work effectively within deadlines and manage multiple responsibilities;
  • Strong commitment to collegiality, teamwork, and a positive academic environment.

Applicants who meet the above requirements are invited to submit their CV to economics@khazar.org indication “Director, Department of Economics and management”

Application deadline: October 9, 2026

Only shortlisted candidates whose qualifications meet the requirements of the position will be contacted for further recruitment process.

 

Coordinator, Quality Assurance Center 

Key Responsibilities 

  • Prepare quality assurance documents, coordinate related activities with relevant academic and administrative units, and conduct the necessary analyses; 
  • Collect and analyze statistical data on the teaching and learning process and prepare relevant reports; 
  • Design and administer stakeholder surveys, analyze the findings, and prepare comprehensive reports; 
  • Monitor the teaching activities of newly recruited faculty members each semester, as well as the monitoring of midterm and final examinations; 
  • Review academic documents submitted by schools, departments, offices, and other structural units, and provide guidance to ensure their compliance with established requirements; 
  • Prepare internal regulatory documents required for the University’s institutional accreditation; 
  • Contribute to other activities and projects undertaken by the Quality Assurance Center.

  • A master’s degree in education, management, or a related field; 
  • At least one year of professional experience in the education sector; 
  • Sound knowledge of the Law of the Republic of Azerbaijan on Education and the State Strategy for the Development of Education; 
  • Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office applications; 
  • Excellent written communication, interpersonal, and organizational skills; 
  • Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a team; 
  • Fluency in written and spoken Azerbaijani; 
  • Intermediate proficiency in written and spoken English. 

Applicants who meet the above requirements are invited to submit their CVs to quality@khazar.org, indicating “Coordinator, Quality Assurance Center” in the subject line. 

Only shortlisted candidates whose qualifications meet the requirements of the position will be contacted. 

Application deadline: 8 September 2026 

 

Vacancy Announcement: AI Simulation Lab Coordinator

Employer: Khazar University

Position Overview

Khazar University invites qualified applications for the position of AI Simulation Lab Coordinator within the Master’s in AI in Business program. This role is designed for an innovative professional who can bridge the gap between artificial intelligence concepts and real-world business applications. The coordinator will lead hands-on laboratory sessions, showing students how AI tools, dynamic simulations, and data-driven models transform decision-making and business operations.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and lead practical lab sessions demonstrating how AI technologies are applied in various business functions.
  • Oversee lab software, AI tools, simulation environments, and technical platforms used in the Master’s curriculum.
  • Guide master’s students through applied projects, hands-on assignments, and simulation-based case studies.
  • Collaborate with program faculty to align practical lab exercises with theoretical course modules.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence, Business Analytics, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a closely related field.
  • Strong interdisciplinary understanding of both artificial intelligence techniques and practical business management principles.
  • Hands-on experience with modern AI tools, simulation environments, or business intelligence platforms.
  • Excellent presentation and instruction skills to demonstrate complex technical concepts clearly to business-oriented learners.

How to Apply

Interested candidates should submit their CV via email to atalibli@khazar.org with the subject line " AI Simulation Lab Coordinator" by September 7, 2026.

Only candidates who successfully pass the initial screening stage will be contacted.
