## 1. Decisions

| Scope | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Free native MVP** (candidate ↔ AISE employer, founder operations) | **GO, conditional on the three host checks in §4.4** | Every local journey passes on this candidate: signup and verification from captured mail, CV → profile, matching, tailored CV, native application with files, employer review, and founder operations with MFA. - `scripts/smoke-e2e.sh` grants its test plan and approves its company directly in the database. It is API coverage, not proof of mailbox delivery, the admin UI or payments.
