## 12. Touch-target accessibility pass (session 4, commit `a545bb4`)
**Measurement method (not CSS classes).** `apps/api/test/support/target-audit.ts` scrolls every visible interactive control into view in Chromium and sweeps `document.elementFromPoint` outward from its centre along both axes; the control's hit area is how far it (or a descendant, e.g. an expanded `::after`) keeps owning the point. A neighbour that covers part of a control therefore *shrinks* its measured area, so overlap cannot hide. Inline links inside running text are exempt (WCAG 2.5.8 inline exception) and counted separately; disabled and visually hidden controls are skipped; checkboxes/radios are measured through their wrapping label. `test/touch-audit-browser.ts` runs it over **38 screens at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px** (152 screen×width combinations, ~2,800 control measurements): public (landing, jobs, pricing, login/register, employer login, contact, a job page), candidate (dashboard, saved with a saved job and search, companies, matches, applications, notifications with a posting alert, settings, profile, resumes, apply), employer (dashboard, vacancies, new/edit, applications, application detail, company, settings) and all 12 admin pages (SUPER_ADMIN with a second session, a second operator and an invitation so row actions exist; a pending company so approve/reject exist). It also fails on any horizontal overflow.
**Before:** 893 observations = **295 distinct controls** under 44 px in 67 shared-cause groups, e.g. footer links 18 px high (174), the brand logo link 32 px high on every signed-in and public page, inputs/selects 38–43 px, card title links 20 px, mobile menu button 40 px, 36 px icon buttons in the vacancy table, filter pills 29 px, legal-page navigation links 19 px.
**Fixes (shared causes once):** global `min-height: 44px` for inputs and selects; footer links; the brand logo hit box (`BrandLogo`, plus the landing module's logo, scroll-hint and employer link); `tap` / `tap-inline` utility classes (in `globals.css`) applied only to `<a>`, `<Link>`, `<button>` and `<label>` elements that were short (94 elements in 32 files; 152 further matches that landed on non-control elements were detected and reverted); icons keep their size and only the hit box grows; the contact table now scrolls inside its own region at 320 px (it overflowed by 50 px).
**After:** **0 controls under 44 px, 0 horizontal overflow**, 20 inline text links exempt. Two cases the first audit data did not contain were found by the older heuristic check and fixed (the admin "Digərlərini bağla" button, enabled only with ≥ 2 sessions, and the saved-job title link), after which the audit data was widened to include those states.
**Regression checks:** web `tsc` clean, Vitest 295 pass, `next build --webpack` compiles; `admin-mobile-browser.ts` (21 checks: health states, dialog focus and busy state, roles, expired session, 12 admin pages × 4 widths, the 390 px candidate/employer journey and 19 screens × 3 widths) passed on the first pass; focus-visible styles, keyboard behaviour, reduced-motion handling and the confirmation dialog are untouched.

The audit measures the pointer hit box, not spacing between adjacent targets beyond "does a neighbour cover it". The 44 px rule was applied to standalone controls regardless of pointer type.
