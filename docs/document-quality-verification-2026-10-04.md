Checkpoint audited: `fe1081a2b8b3222be1dac4ba110df5be528c8369` — **CI green on both runs for that exact SHA** (runs 37201763694 and 37201760884). Nothing here touches vacancy acquisition, scoring, payments, deployment or the founder's/Gemini's databases and checkouts.

## 1. Audit of the preservation logic: defects found and fixed
| # | Finding (verified by a failing test or a rendered page) | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The generic fact check accepts a number found **anywhere** in the candidate's facts, so "70%" from role B could be written under role A, and "30%" could become "30 clients" | Role-scoped check (`metrics.ts`): a number in a role's bullet must come from that role's own saved wording and keep its unit (percent / currency / plain); else `misattributed_metric` / `changed_metric_meaning` (errors, bullet removed). Same for a reworded project. Dates, the role's own header and tool names (`Python 3`) may appear as plain numbers |
| 2 | Restoring a lost metric **appended** the saved line, creating a near-duplicate beside the model's rewording | The saved line replaces the bullet that says nearly the same thing; it is added only when nothing similar exists and there is room; never beyond the per-role limit (resume 4, CV 8) |
| 3 | The restored line could be in **another language** than the selected one (saved text English, document Azerbaijani) | Not copied in. `dropped_metric` carries a reason code and the saved line, and the review screen says in Azerbaijani why and that the candidate can add it |
| 4 | A resume that grew past one page was only discovered in the PDF | The draft detail now carries `layout {pages, expectedMaxPages, status}` (resume and letter 1, CV 2); the review screen shows a notice. Nothing is cut or hidden; PDF is unchanged |
| 5 | Parser limits: new suggestions that did not fit under the 150-entry limit vanished silently | `addSuggestionsFromUpload` reports `limited`; the upload's `parsedContent.omitted.careerSuggestions` includes them. A notice (`ExtractionNotice`) in the wizard and on the documents page says what was **not read** and where to add it by hand. This is kept distinct from the generated-document notes (what a model removed or left out) |
| 6 | Review notes mixed removals and cautions under one heading that claimed everything "was not included", and notes were English prose in an Azerbaijani screen | Two sections: "removed" and "notes to check"; each note has an action hint; details are language-neutral (quoted text and numbers) and reasons are codes worded by the app |
| 7 | Empty "SKILLS" heading printed when every skill group was unticked in review; blank rows (empty language, project, certificate, school) could render | Empty groups and blank rows are dropped before rendering |
| 8 | A single very long token (a 90+ character URL, compound string) ran **off the page** and overprinted the date column | Tokens ≥ 70 characters get invisible break points; shorter URLs and all contact fields stay exactly as written for ATS parsing. Verified in a rendered page and by a test that fails without the fix (mutation-checked) |

Verified safe (rendered): markup-looking text (`#set`, `*bold*`, `$x$`, `<label>`, `@ref`, backslashes, emoji) prints literally and is never executed; Cyrillic, Turkish (İ ı ş ğ) and Azerbaijani text with correct upper-cased headings; letters with no recipient, very long paragraphs across a page break with closing and signature intact; a 12-role, 4-page CV breaks cleanly.
Unchanged and re-verified: review, approval, snapshots, request idempotency, allowance accounting (see section 4).

## 3. The real-provider pilot: prepared, **not run, not authorised**
Scope: an Azerbaijani CV, an English resume and an Azerbaijani letter on synthetic candidates, **old prompts (commit 12e3570) versus new on identical facts and vacancy**: 6 generations. `scripts/preview/run-quality-pilot.sh` (harness `--pilot`).
* **Caps:** at most **18 provider requests** (6 generations × the product's 3-attempt bound; typically 6) and **150,000 tokens**, enforced in the harness (it stops between generations at the token cap and aborts at the request cap). Cost: only if the provider's published prices are supplied; otherwise tokens only. I do not know the provider's price or whether the account is free-tier, and I will not assume either.
* **Required configuration, supplied by the operator, never by me:** a file **outside the repository**, mode 600, owned by you, default `~/aise-preview/provider.env`, with `NVIDIA_API_KEY=…` (optionally `NIM_MODEL`, `EVAL_PRICE_IN_PER_MTOK`, `EVAL_PRICE_OUT_PER_MTOK`). The launcher parses only those names (it never executes the file), never prints a value, refuses a loose mode or a file inside the repo, and does not read any production env file.
* **Authorisation is separate from the key:** the launcher refuses to run unless `--authorize-requests N --authorize-tokens M` are given, and logs the authorisation (no secret) to `~/aise-preview/evals/authorizations.log`. Without them it prints the plan and what is missing. Verified: with no file, with a loose-permission file, and with a missing cap it refuses and calls nothing.
* **When run:** every PDF is also rasterised to PNG for visual inspection; the report keeps **automatic flags** (roles kept, metrics kept, invented numbers, filler, skill dumps, bullet counts, overflow pages, surviving personal claims) apart from **human judgements** (blank rubric: factual, specificity, language/terminology, typography), plus `blind-ab-sheet.md` (old/new as anonymous A/B, key in a separate file) and `az-review-sheet.md`. No claim of improved naturalness without an independent native Azerbaijani reader. Expansion to the full run (now 30 generations, 90 requests at the bound, `--arms` / no `--pilot`) only if the pilot works and its results justify it.
* **Status:** no key and no recorded spending authorisation exist in this environment, so **the pilot is blocked**. The fake provider was not used to stand in for it.
