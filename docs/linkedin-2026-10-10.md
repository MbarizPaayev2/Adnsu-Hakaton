# LinkedIn: what is supported, what is not, and why (10 October 2026)

Status: **investigation complete; no LinkedIn scraping, no Easy Apply automation, nothing sent to LinkedIn.**

## Findings
| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Is there an official way for AISE to *read* LinkedIn job postings? | Only through LinkedIn's partner programmes (Job Posting API / Talent Solutions). They are for ATS and job-board partners that post or sync **their own customers'** jobs; access requires an application, a partner agreement and a reviewed use case. AISE is a candidate-facing application, not an ATS. |
| Is there an official way for AISE to *submit* an application on a candidate's behalf? | **Apply Connect** is an employer/ATS integration: it lets an employer's ATS receive applications that candidates start on LinkedIn. It is not permission for a third party to submit arbitrary candidate applications. |
| Can AISE read LinkedIn through the candidate's browser session? | That is automated access to LinkedIn pages and Easy Apply forms, which LinkedIn's User Agreement prohibits (scraping, bots, browser extensions that automate). It is not implemented and will not be. |

## What AISE does instead
1. **Documents for a LinkedIn vacancy the candidate pastes.** The candidate copies the vacancy title, company and text into AISE; AISE prepares the CV/letter exactly as for any other vacancy (evidence-first, fact-checked, candidate-approved) and the candidate applies on LinkedIn **manually**. *Not built yet in this release:* the letter composer's manual-entry step produces a short honest local draft only; a full "private vacancy" path (hidden Job + Match so the whole generation, allowance and approval flow applies) is the next step and is listed under limitations.
2. **The same vacancies from the employer's own sources.** Where an employer publishes on its own career page or ATS (Lever, Workable, Teamtailor, SAP SuccessFactors, the employer's own feed), AISE collects it from there under the source-review rules in `docs/employer-coverage-2026-10-10.md`. That is the supported route to many of the postings that also appear on LinkedIn.
3. **Applications by e-mail** where the employer **explicitly published** an application address (never inferred from a generic contact).

## What would change this
A LinkedIn partner agreement. If AISE ever becomes an approved Talent Solutions partner, the integration would be built against the official API and would be described separately; until then there is nothing to switch on.
