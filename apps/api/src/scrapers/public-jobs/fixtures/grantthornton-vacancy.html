We are looking for a Payroll and HR Specialist to join our growing team and support a diverse portfolio of clients across multiple industries. 
This is an excellent opportunity for a detail-oriented and motivated professional who is eager to deepen their expertise in a dynamic audit and advisory environment.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Manage end-to-end payroll processes across multiple client accounts;
  • Ensure accurate and timely payroll calculation in accordance with Azerbaijani labor and tax legislation;
  • Maintain and update data in the EMAS system, ensuring full compliance  and accuracy;
  • Prepare and submit required reports to government authorities;
  • Support HR administrative tasks for clients, including onboarding, contract preparation, and personnel documentation;
  • Communicate with clients on payroll and HR matters in a professional and timely manner;
  • Maintain confidentiality and adhere to professional ethics at all times.

Requirements:

  • Azerbaijani national;
  • Minimum 2 years of relevant experience in payroll processing;
  • Proven hands-on experience working with the EMAS system;
  • Previous experience in an audit, outsourcing, or advisory firm is highly preferred;
  • Strong command of Azerbaijani (native/proficient);
  • Intermediate or higher English proficiency;
  • Strong attention to detail, analytical mindset, and commitment to accuracy;
  • Ability to work with multiple clients and manage deadlines effectively.

What We Offer:

  • Opportunity to work within a reputable professional services environment;
  • Exposure to diverse clients and industries;
  • Supportive team culture and opportunities for professional growth;
  • Competitive compensation package

How to Apply: If you meet the above criteria and are ready to take the next step in your career, please send your CV to info@az.gt.com or click the "Apply to this position" button above.
